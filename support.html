IVE on the iPhone

The iPhone app is a companion to IVE on your Mac. Your sessions, your code and the coding agents stay on the Mac. The phone shows them, lets you answer and lets you start new work.

You need the Mac app first. It is free: Download IVE for Mac.

No Mac nearby? Tap Try the demo on the first screen of the app. It shows sample sessions on the phone and sends nothing anywhere.

Connect the phone to your Mac

1. Open IVE on your Mac.
2. Open Phone in the sidebar and choose Start secure connection.
3. Choose Show pairing code.
4. In the iPhone app, tap Scan QR code and point the camera at the code. You can also paste the pairing link.

A pairing code works once and expires after five minutes. If it expired, show a new one.

The app cannot reach my Mac

The Mac has to be awake and IVE has to be open. A sleeping Mac does not answer.

The secure connection has to be running. When it restarts it gets a new address, and the phone needs a new pairing code. Open Phone in IVE on the Mac, start the secure connection and pair again.

On the same Wi-Fi as the Mac, iOS may ask for Local Network access. Allow it under Settings, Privacy & Security, Local Network, IVE.

Remove a phone

In the iPhone app, a two-finger double tap opens the settings. Choose Pair again or change Mac. The app then forgets the Mac and its sign-in.

To revoke the phone completely, also remove it under Devices in IVE on the Mac.

Face ID lock

The app can lock itself with Face ID or your device passcode. Switch it on or off in the app settings (two-finger double tap) and choose how long the app may stay in the background before it asks again.

IVE on the Mac

IVE runs on Macs with Apple silicon and macOS 13.5 or newer. The app is signed by AIFOREU GmbH and notarized by Apple. Installation and updates are described on the download page.
